# Moodboard: SkillSwap, 30 seconds

Low fidelity on purpose. This is the plan the brief and the scenes are written from: what the film should feel like, look like and sound like, before anything is animated.

## Feeling

Calm, confident, a little playful. One idea at a time, small type, lots of dark blue air. It should feel like one continuous thought, not a slideshow.

## Reference

`.claude/skills/motion-video/references/smooth-2d-saas.md`. Taken from it: one static backdrop, word-by-word sentences, one element surviving every cut, a list on a wheel, a ring of cards, a mark that assembles into the lockup. Not taken: its logo, wording or images.

## Look

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Backdrop | Deep navy `#050A30` with soft blue glows top-centre and bottom-right. Never changes. |
| Type | White, regular weight, about 8% of frame height, slightly tight tracking. |
| Accent | Amber `#F59E0B` for "swap" and the mark. Light amber `#FCD34D` as a passing tint. |
| Quiet text | `#8FB0FF` for list lines that are not active. |
| Success | Green `#10B981`, only on "Matched". |
| Surfaces | White and pale blue cards with deep-blue text. |
| Icons | Thin white line icons, one per skill. |

## Rough frames

One line per beat: what the frame holds when it is at rest.

| # | Time | Frame at rest | What survives into the next beat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 to 4 s | `What if learning was a [swap?]` on one line, "swap?" in amber | The whole sentence |
| 2 | 4 to 8 s | `[swap]` alone in the centre, six skill pills in two rows trading places around it | "swap", centred |
| 3 | 8 to 12 s | `[swap] what you know to learn.` then only `learn •` at the left | "learn" and the bullet |
| 4 | 12 to 16.5 s | `learn • Guitar` with other skills fanned above and below on a wheel | Nothing: a short breath |
| 5 | 16.5 to 22.5 s | A ring of matched-swap cards turning in 3D, "Real people. Live 1:1 sessions." beneath | Nothing: a short breath |
| 6 | 22.5 to 26 s | A white ring and an amber disc trading places | The two circles, as the mark |
| 7 | 26 to 30 s | Mark, `Skill[Swap]`, and "Learn. Teach. Grow together." | End |

## Sound

Music carries the calm; effects are small and dry, and only mark something the eye already sees.

**Music.** "Deliberate Thought" by Kevin MacLeod: a soft, steady electronic bed. Enters at 0 s with a half-second fade, sits under everything, and fades over the last 1.5 s. The track is quiet at its start, so it plays at 80% of its own level.

**Effects.**

| Moment | Sound | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each word of the opening sentence landing | Soft click, quiet | Gives the word-by-word build a pulse |
| "swap" gliding to the centre, and later to the start of the line | Short whoosh | Marks the one big move of each scene |
| Each pair of pills trading places | Switch | The trade is the idea of the product; it should be heard |
| Full stop becoming the bullet | Small whip | A tiny flourish for the hand-off |
| Each step of the skill wheel | Switch, quieter | Keeps the 0.5 s beat audible |
| Wheel spinning away | Whip | Release |
| Card ring swinging in and lifting out | Whoosh | Size and weight |
| The mark's two trades | Whip | Echoes the pill trades |
| Wordmark wiping on | Whoosh, soft | Arrival |
| Tagline landing | Ding | The one bright sound, saved for the end |

Exact times are in the Sound table of `prompts/skillswap-30.md`.

## Open questions

- The music and effects were chosen from their descriptions and checked for length and format. Nobody has listened to the mix yet. Levels were set from measurement only: the first render averaged about -33 dB and was raised.
