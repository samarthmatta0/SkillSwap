# SkillSwap promo, 20 seconds

Make a 20-second launch video for SkillSwap, a platform where people trade skills in live 1:1 sessions: you teach something you know and learn something you want.

- Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps, 600 frames. No audio, no voiceover, so every idea has to read from the screen alone.
- Audience: people who want to learn a practical skill and have one of their own to offer.
- Tone: warm, confident and clear. Friendly rather than corporate. Clean shapes and generous white space, no clutter.

## Brand

- Deep blue `#1E3A8A` (primary), bright blue `#3B82F6`, dark blue `#1E40AF`
- Amber `#F59E0B` (accent, use sparingly for emphasis), light amber `#FCD34D`
- Green `#10B981` only for a "matched" or success moment
- Background white `#FFFFFF` or light grey `#F9FAFB`; body text `#1F2937`
- Wordmark: the word "SkillSwap" in heavy weight, "Skill" in deep blue and "Swap" in amber. There is no logo file, so set it as text.

## Scenes

1. **Hook (0-4 s).** White background. The headline "Learn any skill." builds in word by word in deep blue. A beat later, a second line "Teach what you know." in amber rises under it.
2. **The swap (4-9 s).** Two rounded cards slide in from the left and right. The left card reads "I teach: Guitar" and "I want: Spanish"; the right card reads "I teach: Spanish" and "I want: Guitar". Two curved arrows draw between them to form a loop, and a small green "Matched" pill pops in at the centre.
3. **How it works (9-14 s).** Three numbered steps appear in a row, staggered: "1 List your skills", "2 Get matched", "3 Meet 1:1". A thin bright-blue line draws through the three step numbers to connect them.
4. **Proof of range (14-17 s).** A loose cloud of skill chips pops in with a stagger: Guitar, Spanish, React, Photography, Cooking, Yoga, Public speaking, Excel. Chips alternate between blue outline and amber fill. Over them, the line "Real people. Real skills." settles in.
5. **End card (17-20 s).** Deep-blue full-frame background wipes in. The "SkillSwap" wordmark scales up in white and amber, with the line "Learn. Teach. Grow together." below it in white. Hold the final frame still for the last 1.5 seconds.

Carry the blue from scene to scene: the connecting line in scene 3 and the chip outlines in scene 4 should feel like the same stroke, and the end card's wipe should grow out of it.
